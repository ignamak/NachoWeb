Virtuos Games - Black Shamrock

2025 - Present, Technical Designer (Freelance)

I work as a freelance technical designer on an unannounced AAA action title, designing, implementing and tuning combat AI for regular enemies found in open-world encounters, for bosses, and for an allied NPC that fights alongside the player. I tune perception, target selection, attack, cooldown and difficulty parameters through a data-driven setup. I implement many NPC skills end to end with animation, VFX and audio, and rework and fix many others, working with QA on a daily basis to identify and fix issues in NPC behaviour and skills.

The project is under NDA, so the client and the characters are not named here. See a short NDA-safe overview in the Portfolio.

Lost Criteria Studios (Blackstorm)

2019/2020 - Technical Designer, Game Designer & Sound Designer - Unreal / Blueprints

Lost Criteria Studios is the studio we formed, 18 classmates from the Master's Degree in Game Design at U-tad, to make Blackstorm. It started as a Master's project and we finished it outside the program. The game is a first-person Battle Royale focused on cooperative teamplay, with unique mechanics like multiple gravities in an online game and three axis space navigation. Being part of an interdisciplinary 18 people team taught me how to work in a bigger project where many things are out of your reach.

I worked as game designer (writing design documents for several features, including the inventory, and designing the cannon/weapon system and the behavior of each weapon), as technical designer (implementing gameplay features and systems in Unreal Engine 4, including prototypes of multiplayer mechanics) and as sound designer. We ran live playtests with players at industry fairs. Blackstorm was released on Steam on 1 June 2021, and won PlayStation Talents Spain (Best Press Game) and Fun & Serious (New Talents Prize) in 2019.
